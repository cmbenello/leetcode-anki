How it picks

It scores every free LeetCode problem you haven’t solved (about 3,300) by the chance you’d solve it without help, and aims for about 75%: hard enough to learn from, easy enough to finish. It estimates your skill per topic from every attempt, counting peeks at the editorial as misses. It favours your weakest topics, and an hour to a week after a miss it brings back a similar problem you’re likely to pass. With a goal selected, it stays on that goal. Ranking takes a few milliseconds. The model is ported from Drill.
